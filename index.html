The challenge for this season

Override is this season's game: 15 seconds of autonomous, then 1:45 of driving, on a 12-foot field. A new power rule makes the drivetrain a budget problem first.

  1. Drivetrain motors are capped at 55 watts. Rule R11.
  2. The whole robot gets 88 watts total. Rule R10.
  3. Every watt spent on speed is a watt the arm does not get.

That tradeoff is the season. Sponsors let the team build what it chose, not what it could afford.

The robot shown is Flex, the Hero Bot VEX publishes as a starting platform. This team did not design it.

How to sponsor

This page collects no money. Giving goes through the school district and starts with an email.

Suggested giving levels toward a $15,000 season goal.

Each level includes everything in the level before it.

Aluminum, fasteners, wheels: the parts that get used up and replaced.

  • Your name and logo on every promotional piece the team makes, including this website.

Funds a major mechanism for one of the four teams.

Everything in Stock, plus

  • Your logo on the cover and inside of the team's engineering notebook.
  • Your logo on the team shirts. You choose which of the four teams.

Goes toward parts, entry fees, and field equipment.

Everything in Subsystem, plus

  • Your logo and slogan on the team's pit booth at every competition.

Email a coach about an in-kind gift

  1. Email a coach

    Say you want to sponsor, and whether you are offering cash or materials.

  2. They send you the tax-exempt ID

    The coach replies with the EPISD tax-exempt ID and giving instructions.

  3. The gift goes to Chapin High School

    Earmarked for the robotics program.

The robotics program does not hold its own nonprofit status. It operates under El Paso Independent School District, and a gift is made to Chapin High School and earmarked for the robotics program. The district's tax-exempt identification number is not published on this page. A coach will send it to you by email. Confirm the treatment of your own gift with your accountant.
